## What the TNPSC Portal Requires

TNPSC registration requires two separate photo uploads:

**Photograph:** JPG format, 20 KB to 50 KB, white background, passport-size

**Signature:** JPG format, 10 KB to 20 KB, blue/black ink on white paper

Your smartphone camera produces photos between 3,000 KB and 8,000 KB. The TNPSC portal maximum is 50 KB for photos. That's a gap of 60 to 160 times. You absolutely need a compression tool — and the right one makes the difference between a blurry, rejected upload and a perfectly compressed, portal-ready image.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I already registered for TNPSC. Can I update my photo using this tool?
You can use the tool to compress a new photo. Whether you can update the photo on TNPSC depends on whether an editing window is currently open. Check tnpsc.gov.in for the latest information.

### The tool says "compress to 50KB" but TNPSC allows 20-50KB. Is targeting 50KB safe?
Yes — "compress to 50KB" means the result will be just under 50 KB (typically 35-48 KB), which is within the 20-50 KB required range. We specifically calibrate to be safely under the stated maximum.

### After compressing, my photo shows as 47.3 KB. Will the portal accept this?
Yes — 47.3 KB is under 50 KB, so it will be accepted by any portal with a 50 KB maximum. Portals check whether the file is under the maximum, not the exact precise number.

### I don't have a white background for my photo. Can I take the photo first and then remove the background?
There are background removal tools online. However, for TNPSC, it's much better to take the photo with a proper white background from the start — background removal tools sometimes leave artifacts around the hair that can be detected by automated systems. Take the photo correctly from the beginning.
