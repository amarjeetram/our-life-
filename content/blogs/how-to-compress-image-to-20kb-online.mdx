## How to Compress Image to 20kb (The Super Small Size!)

Okay, so you want to send a picture for a form, and they said, "**Reduce image size to 20kb**." That is super tiny! Here is how you do it.

1. **Go to a Website:** Open a free compressor website.
2. **Upload:** Click the button that says "Upload Image." Pick the picture from your gallery. Maybe a selfie or a picture of your pet.
3. **Look for Settings:** On most websites, you will see a slider or a box where you can type a number. This is the fun part!
4. **Type the Number:** Look for an option that says "Target Size" or "Desired Size." Type **20** in the box. Make sure it says **kb**.
5. **Let it Work:** Click the button that says "Compress" or "Resize." The website will do the magic for you.
6. **Download:** After a few seconds, it will say "Done!" There will be a button that says "Download." Click it. Now you have a picture that is exactly **20 kb**!

See? That was easy. You just learned how to **compress image to 20kb online**.
