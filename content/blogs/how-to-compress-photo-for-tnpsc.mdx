Applying for a TNPSC exam is an exciting step toward a government career in Tamil Nadu. But the online registration process can be stressful — especially when you hit technical errors during the photo upload step.

This practical guide focuses on exactly how to compress your photo and signature to meet TNPSC specifications, and how to avoid the most common upload errors.





## The Two Upload Steps That Trip People Up

During TNPSC online registration, you'll need to upload:

1. **Passport Photograph** — 20 KB to 50 KB, JPG format, white background
2. **Signature** — 10 KB to 20 KB, JPG format, white paper background

Both must be uploaded successfully before you can submit the application. And both have strict file size limits that your regular phone camera photos will NOT meet without compression.

## Step 1: Getting Your Passport Photo Ready

### Option A: Studio Photo (Recommended)

Visit a professional photo studio and ask for:
- A white background passport photo
- The digital file on a USB or sent via WhatsApp

Ask specifically: *"Mujhe digital passport photo chahiye JPG format mein white background ke saath"* (or in Tamil: *"Ennakku digital passport photo venum, JPG format la, vella background la"*)

The studio will give you a high-quality 2-3 MB JPG file. Now you need to compress it.

### Option B: Phone Photo at Home

1. Stand against a plain white wall in bright natural light
2. Have a friend take the photo at face level
3. Check — is the background pure white? Is your face clearly visible?
4. Choose the sharpest photo from several attempts

### Compressing the Photo

Open SmartToolsWala in your phone's browser. Go to the TNPSC compressor or "Compress Image to 50KB" tool. Upload your photo. Download the compressed result (should be 35-48 KB).

**Verify before uploading:**
- ✅ File is .jpg format
- ✅ File size is between 20 KB and 50 KB
- ✅ Face is clearly visible
- ✅ Background is white

## Step 2: Getting Your Signature Ready

### The Signature Photo

1. Sign on a clean white A4 paper with a blue ballpoint pen
2. Sign slightly larger than your normal signature
3. Place paper on flat table, photograph from directly above
4. Use natural daylight — NO flash, no artificial light if possible
5. Crop in Gallery to show just the signature with small white borders

### Compressing the Signature

Use SmartToolsWala's "Compress Image to 20KB" tool. Upload your cropped signature. Download the compressed result (should be 12-18 KB).

**Verify before uploading:**
- ✅ File is .jpg format
- ✅ File size is between 10 KB and 20 KB
- ✅ Signature lines are dark and clearly visible
- ✅ Background is white (not grey or yellow)

## Common TNPSC Upload Error Messages and Solutions

### Error: "File size exceeds maximum limit"
Your file is too large. Use SmartToolsWala to compress to the correct size.

### Error: "Invalid file type. Please upload JPG"
Your file might be saved as .png, .heic, or .webp. Our compression tool outputs JPG automatically. Make sure the file ends in .jpg when you save it.

### Error: "Image is not a valid photo. Please reupload."
This sometimes happens if the portal detects an image that doesn't look like a passport photo (wrong dimensions ratio or the image is very dark). Make sure you're uploading a proper passport-style photo, not a landscape or casual photo.

### Error: "Minimum file size not met"
If the portal requires a minimum of 20 KB for photos and your file is only 15 KB:
- Start with a higher-resolution original photo (take a new photo with maximum camera quality)
- The more pixels in the original, the easier it is to stay above 20 KB while still compressing below 50 KB

### Portal freezing or getting stuck during upload
Try using Chrome on a desktop/laptop computer. TNPSC portals are sometimes slow on mobile browsers, especially during peak registration periods.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The TNPSC website keeps showing my photo as low quality even though MY phone displays it clearly. Why?
The portal may use a different display method than your phone. Trust the compressed file quality — if the compressed file looks clear when you open it directly on your device, it will be fine for the application. Don't rely on how the portal thumbnail shows it.

### Can I use the same photo for Group 1 and Group 4 applications?
Yes, as long as the photo is recent (usually within 6 months), meets the current requirements for both exams, and is within the specified file size.

### I submitted the application but the photo looks different than expected now. Can I fix it?
Before the final submission deadline, contact TNPSC's help desk or look for a "Correction Window" announcement on the official website. Some exam authorities open brief periods for candidates to correct uploaded documents.
