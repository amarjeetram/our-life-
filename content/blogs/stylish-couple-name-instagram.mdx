## Why Combine Names?
1. **Wedding Hashtags:** Modern weddings require a unique `#hashtag` for friends and family to upload pictures. A combined name is easiest to remember.
2. **Social Media Bios:** Cute couple accounts or shared profiles need a unified identity.
3. **Nicknames:** It’s a fun, affectionate way to address each other.
