## Understanding Different Photo Formats

### JPG (JPEG)
The most common photo format. Used by most Android cameras, all older iPhones, and digital cameras. Compresses very efficiently. Ideal for photos with many colors (faces, landscapes).

**Portal requirement:** Most government portals specifically require JPG.

### PNG
Common for screenshots and design files. PNG is a "lossless" format — no quality is ever lost when saving. But this means PNG files are much larger than JPG for the same photo. A PNG photo might be 3-4x the size of the same JPG.

**Problem:** PNG doesn't compress as efficiently to tiny sizes like 100 KB.

### HEIC (HEIF)
Apple's modern format, used by iPhones since 2017. HEIC files are very small (smaller than JPG) while maintaining high quality. However, many government portals don't accept HEIC — they only accept JPG.

**Problem:** HEIC is not accepted by most portals. Must be converted.

### WEBP
Google's modern image format, common on websites. Very efficient compression but not universally accepted by portals.

### BMP
An older, uncompressed format. BMP files are enormous — a BMP photo from a camera might be 50-100 MB. Definitely must be converted before any online form submission.
