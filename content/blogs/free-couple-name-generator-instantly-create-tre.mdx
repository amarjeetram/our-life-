## The History of “Ship” Names: Why Are They So Popular?
Why do we love mashing names together? It all started in the world of celebrity fandoms and internet culture.

### 1. The “Bennifer” Effect
The concept exploded in the early 2000s with Ben Affleck and Jennifer Lopez. The media dubbed them “Bennifer,” and the world went crazy. It was catchy, short, and instantly recognizable.

### 2. The Rise of “Shipping”
The term “Shipping” comes from the word “Relationship.” Fans of TV shows and movies started creating names for characters they wanted to see together.
- Harry Potter fans had “Dramione” (Draco + Hermione).
- Friends fans rooted for “Roschel” (Ross + Rachel).

### 3. The Indian Craze (Virushka & DeepVeer)
In India, the trend reached new heights with celebrity weddings. When Virat Kohli and Anushka Sharma got married, #Virushka trended for weeks. It wasn’t just a name; it became a brand. Today, almost every couple wants that same “celebrity feel” for their own relationship.

## Tips for Choosing the Perfect Couple Name
Our tool will give you dozens of options. How do you pick the best one? Here is a checklist:

### 1. The “Sound” Test
Say the name out loud. Does it roll off the tongue easily?
- **Good:** “Saifeena” (Smooth)
- **Bad:** “Saikareena” (Clunky)

### 2. Avoid Weird Meanings
Check if the combined name accidentally spells something else.
- **Example:** Brad + Angelina = “Brangelina” (Good).
- **Caution:** If your names are “Hit” and “Man”, be careful not to make “Hitman” unless you want to sound dangerous!

### 3. Ask Your Friends
Narrow it down to your top 3 favorites and put up a poll on your Instagram Story. Let your friends decide!

### 4. Who is the “Dominant” Name?
Usually, the name with stronger vowels starts the ship name. However, try to find a balance where both names are represented equally.
