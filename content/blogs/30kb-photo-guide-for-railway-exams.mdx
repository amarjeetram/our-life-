Indian Railways is one of the largest employers in the world, offering thousands of government jobs every year through RRB (Railway Recruitment Board) exams like NTPC, Group D, and ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot).

Millions of candidates apply for these exams. And every single one of them must navigate the online registration process — including the all-important photo upload step.

This guide is specifically written for railway exam aspirants, covering exactly what photos are required, what specifications to follow, and how to compress your photo to the required size quickly.





## Railway Exam Photo Specifications (General Reference)

**Note:** Specifications can change between exam cycles. Always verify with the current official RRB notification PDF before applying.

| Requirement | Typical Specification |
|---|---|
| Photo Format | JPG/JPEG |
| Photo File Size | 20 KB to 50 KB (for most RRB exams) |
| Signature File Size | 10 KB to 30 KB |
| Background | White |
| Photo Type | Recent passport size (within 6 months) |
| Dimensions | Approximately 200×230 pixels |

## The Two Photos Required for RRB Registration

### 1. Passport Photograph
A standard passport-size photograph showing your face clearly against a white background. Requirements:
- Plain white background (not blue, grey, or patterned)
- Clear frontal view of face
- Natural expression — slight smile acceptable, not wide smile
- No dark spectacles
- Uniform or decent clothing (not very casual)
- Taken within the last 6 months

### 2. Signature
Your normal handwritten signature on white paper. Requirements:
- Signed with blue or black pen
- On plain white paper
- Photographed clearly (no blur, no shadows)
- No printed name alongside — just signature

## How to Prepare Railway Exam Photos at Home

### Passport Photo

**Location:** Find a room with a large window. Natural afternoon light works best.

**Setup:** Stand 1 to 1.5 meters from a plain white wall. Do not stand too close — you don't want your shadow on the wall.

**Camera:** Use the rear camera of your smartphone for better quality than the front camera. Ask a friend to take the photo, or use a phone stand with the timer.

**Check:** After taking, open the photo and zoom in to 150%. Your eyes should be sharp, the background should be evenly white, and the lighting should be even on both sides of your face.

### Signature

**Paper:** Use a white A4 sheet with no lines, patterns, or printing.

**Pen:** Blue ballpoint pen (like Reynolds 045 or Cello Gripper). Avoid pencil, gel pen, or marker.

**Sign:** Write your signature slightly larger than normal, centered on the paper.

**Photo:** Place paper on flat table. Hold phone directly above. Natural window light. NO flash. Tap screen to focus on signature. Take photo.

**Crop in Gallery:** Crop to show just the signature with small space around it.

## Common Railway Portal Upload Errors

### "File size too large"
Your photo exceeds 50 KB or signature exceeds 30 KB. Use SmartToolsWala to compress to the correct size.

### "Invalid file format"
Your file is not a JPG. Make sure the file extension is .jpg (lowercase). Our tool automatically outputs JPG format.

### "Image resolution/quality too low"
The portal's automated system detected a low-quality image. This happens when:
- The original photo was very dark (poor lighting)
- The original photo was blurry (camera shake)
- You compressed an already-compressed WhatsApp photo

Fix: Retake the original in bright natural light with a steady hand.

### "This image does not appear to be a valid photo"
Usually means the portal doesn't recognize the image as a passport photo. Ensure:
- Face is clearly visible and frontal
- Background is white (not dark or colorful)
- Image dimensions are within the required range

## Timeline: When to Prepare Your Photos

Don't leave photo preparation until the last minute! Here's a recommended timeline:

**2-3 days before the deadline:**
- Take your passport photo
- Take your signature photo
- Compress both to the required sizes
- Verify all specifications are met

**Day before the deadline:**
- Have both files ready on your device
- Complete the registration form up to the photo upload step
- Upload photos and complete registration

**Why so early?** Railway exam portals experience extreme traffic near deadlines. Slow loading times, server errors, and payment gateway issues are common in the last 24 hours. Preparing early means you can handle technical errors with time to spare.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I prepared my photos months before the exam. Can I still use them during registration?
Most RRB notifications require photos taken within the **last 6 months**. If your photos are older, retake a fresh set to comply with this requirement.

### Do I need to get my photo attested by a gazetted officer?
No. For online registration, you upload a digital photo — no physical attestation is required. Attestation might be required later when you submit physical documents for verification, but not for online registration itself.

### My photo was taken at a studio with a blue background (like my Aadhaar photo). Can I use it?
RRB requires a **white background**. A blue background photo will likely be rejected. Take a fresh photo with a white background specifically for the application.

### Can I use a JPEG photo from my Aadhaar card for the railway exam?
No — the photo on your Aadhaar card is already a small, compressed image taken years ago. Railway regulations require a recent (within 6 months) photograph. Use a freshly taken photo.
