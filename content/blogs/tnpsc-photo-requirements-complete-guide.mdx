Every year, lakhs of candidates from Tamil Nadu apply for TNPSC (Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission) exams. The TNPSC exams are the gateway to prestigious government jobs in Tamil Nadu — Group 1, Group 2, Group 2A, Group 4, and many others.

But thousands of candidates face the same problem during online registration: **photo and signature upload errors.** The TNPSC portal has very specific requirements, and even a small mistake — like uploading a 55 KB photo when the limit is 50 KB — will block your application.

This complete guide covers every aspect of TNPSC photo and signature requirements so you can register without any problems.





## TNPSC Photo Requirements (Current)

| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| File Format | JPG/JPEG only |
| File Size | 20 KB to 50 KB |
| Dimensions | 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (standard passport size) |
| Background | White or off-white |
| Image Type | Passport-style photograph |
| Recency | Taken within last 6 months |

## TNPSC Signature Requirements (Current)

| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| File Format | JPG/JPEG only |
| File Size | 10 KB to 20 KB |
| Background | White paper |
| Ink | Blue or black pen |
| Content | Full signature only (no printed name) |

**Important note:** TNPSC periodically updates these specifications. Always verify the current requirements on the official TNPSC website (tnpsc.gov.in) for the specific exam you are applying for.

## Step-by-Step: How to Prepare Your TNPSC Passport Photo

### The Photo
Your TNPSC passport photo must meet these visual requirements:
- **Face:** Clearly visible, looking directly at the camera
- **Expression:** Neutral — no smiling broadly, no frowning
- **Head covering:** Not required (religious head coverings are allowed)
- **Spectacles:** Permitted if you normally wear them, but no dark glasses
- **Background:** Plain white preferred

### The Lighting
Natural daylight near a window is best. Your face should be evenly lit — no dark shadows on one side. Professional studios with proper lighting are also an excellent choice for TNPSC applications.

### The Format
Your photo must come from a **passport-size photograph**. You can:
1. Go to a professional photo studio and ask for a digital copy of your passport photo (they'll give you a JPG file)
2. Take one at home using a smartphone (see method below)

### Home Photography Method
1. Stand against a plain white wall
2. Position a chair or phone stand so the camera is at face level
3. Face the camera directly with natural light falling on your face
4. Take with good lighting — daytime near a window
5. Take 10 photos and select the sharpest, best-lit one

## How to Compress TNPSC Signature to 10-20 KB

### Taking the Signature Photo

1. Sign your name (as you would sign on official documents) on a clean white paper using a **blue ballpoint pen**
2. Place the paper on a flat table near a window
3. Hold your phone directly above the paper (overhead view)
4. Focus the camera on the signature by tapping the screen
5. Take the photo in bright natural daylight (no flash)
6. Crop in your phone's gallery to show just the signature with a thin white border

## Common TNPSC Registration Photo Errors

### Error: "File size exceeds maximum limit"
**Cause:** Your photo or signature is larger than the maximum allowed size.
**Fix:** Use SmartToolsWala to compress to the correct size.

### Error: "Invalid file format"
**Cause:** Your file is saved as PNG, HEIC, PDF, or another non-JPG format.
**Fix:** Our tool automatically outputs JPG. Make sure your file extension shows .jpg

### Error: "Image dimensions are not valid"
**Cause:** The photo's pixel dimensions don't match requirements.
**Fix:** Use a photo resizer tool to adjust dimensions to the required specification (typically around 200×230 pixels), then compress to the right file size.

### Error: "Image too small (below minimum size)"
**Cause:** Your file is below the 20 KB minimum.
**Fix:** Start with a higher-resolution original photo. The more detail in the original, the easier it is to reach the 20 KB minimum while still compressing below 50 KB.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use the same photo for multiple TNPSC exams?
Yes, as long as the photo was taken within the last 6 months and still meets the current requirements for the exam you're applying to.

### I had my photo taken at a studio but they gave me a file that's 2 MB. What should I do?
Use SmartToolsWala to compress it to 40-48 KB. Studio photos are usually excellent quality and compress beautifully.

### The TNPSC portal keeps giving a generic error when I upload. What should I try?
1. Try a different browser (use Chrome)
2. Try on a computer instead of a phone
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Make sure you're uploading a .jpg file (not .jpeg or .JPG — try lowercase)
5. If all else fails, contact TNPSC helpdesk with a screenshot of the error

### What happens if I accidentally upload the wrong photo?
Before final form submission, most portals allow you to re-upload by clicking the photo area again. If you've already submitted, contact TNPSC's helpdesk immediately — corrections before the last date may be possible.
