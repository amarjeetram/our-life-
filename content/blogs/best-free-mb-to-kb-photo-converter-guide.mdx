## Why Do We Need a Photo Converter?

If this is your first time dealing with computer files, let's do a quick recap.

On the other hand, many websites have very strict rules. If you are applying for a government exam (like UPSC or SSC), or trying to get a job online, the website will have a rule: **"Passport Photo must be under 50 KB."**

## What About Different File Types? (JPG, PNG, WEBP)

While you are managing sizes, you should also pay attention to the three-letter code at the end of your file name. This is called the "file format."

- **.JPG (or .JPEG):** This is the king of photographs. Every phone takes JPGs, and every government website demands JPGs. It compresses incredibly well.
- **.PNG:** This is great for drawings or logos with clear backgrounds. However, PNG files are naturally very heavy! A 2 MB JPG might be a 6 MB PNG. 
- **.WEBP:** This is a brand new format invented by Google. It is super light, but sadly, many old government websites do not accept it yet.
