While 50 KB is the standard for Indian government exam portals, there are many important situations where you need a photo that's **up to 200 KB** — and sometimes exactly 200 KB.

This guide explains every situation where a 200 KB photo is needed and exactly how to compress your photo to meet this requirement.





## Why Some Portals Allow 200 KB (And Why That's Actually Better for You)

When a portal allows 200 KB instead of 50 KB, it's giving you the gift of higher image quality. At 200 KB, your photo will look noticeably better than at 50 KB — more natural skin tones, crisper features, better hair detail.

Here's a practical quality comparison:

| File Size | Quality Level | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 20-30 KB | Acceptable for record keeping | Signatures, some thumb impressions |
| 50 KB | Good — standard government exam quality | All government exam portals |
| 100 KB | Very good — professional job quality | Job applications, college admissions |
| 200 KB | Excellent — near-original quality | Visa applications, bank KYC, professional bodies |
| 500 KB+ | Essentially original quality | High-quality print requirements |

When a portal allows 200 KB, use the full 200 KB limit — don't unnecessarily compress to 50 KB if 200 KB is allowed. Better quality = better representation.

## When You'll Need a 200 KB Photo

### Visa Applications

**US Visa (B1/B2 Tourist/Business):** US visa forms typically accept photos up to 240 KB at 600×600 pixels. Compressing to about 200 KB gives excellent quality.

**UK Visa:** UK visa applications accept photos up to 6 MB, but the platform works best with 200-500 KB.

**Schengen Visa:** Requirements vary by country but most European embassies allow 200 KB to several MB for digital photos.

**Singapore Visa:** Accepts photos up to 2 MB, and 200 KB is an excellent quality-to-size balance.

### Bank Services

**Opening NRI Accounts:** Banks processing NRI (Non-Resident Indian) accounts often require higher-quality photos for regulatory compliance — 200 KB is commonly specified.

**Premium / Private Banking KYC:** Banks upgrading customers to premium accounts may require a fresh, higher-quality photo at 200-500 KB.

**Loan Applications:** Some private banks and NBFCs ask for address proof photos and personal photos at 200 KB for their digital application process.

### Professional Bodies and Registrations

**Bar Council (Lawyers):** State Bar Council online enrollment requires professional photos, usually 200 KB to 500 KB.

**ICAI (Chartered Accountants):** Post-qualification member registration on ICAI's online portal.

**Medical Council (Doctors):** State Medical Council registrations for MBBS/MD graduates.

**Engineering Council:** Some state councils for registered engineers require 200 KB professional photos.

### Society Exams (SSC CGL / CHSL — Skill Test Stage)

Some competitive exams that progress to skill tests or interview stages may update photo requirements for the later stages to allow 200 KB for better quality identification.

### Important Notes for Visa Photos

Visa authorities often have specific requirements beyond just file size. For visa applications, additionally verify:
- **Exact dimensions** (US visa: exactly 600×600 pixels)
- **Background color** (US, Canada: pure white; UK: plain light-colored)
- **Photo recency** (usually within 6 months)
- **Print quality** for physical copies

After compressing to 200 KB, resize to the exact required dimensions if specified using a separate resize tool.

## Common 200 KB Mistakes

### Using Too Small An Original
If you start with a 300 KB original and compress to 200 KB, the result will be poor. Always start from a high-quality 3-10 MB original for the best 200 KB result.

### Forgetting Dimension Requirements
200 KB is the file size, but portals often ALSO specify pixel dimensions (like 600×600). Compress AND resize to meet both requirements.

### Ignoring Background Requirements
Visa photo background requirements are very specific. A wrong background color (even slightly off-white) can cause your visa application to be rejected — regardless of file size.
