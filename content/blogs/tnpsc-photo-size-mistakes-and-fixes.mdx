TNPSC (Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission) exam registration is an important step for lakhs of Tamil Nadu government job aspirants every year. But photo and signature upload errors during registration are incredibly common — and incredibly frustrating.

This guide addresses the most common TNPSC photo size mistakes that candidates make and gives you clear, step-by-step fixes for each one.





## Mistake 1: Uploading a Photo That's Too Large

**The error:** "File size exceeds the maximum limit. Please upload a file under 50 KB."

**Why it happens:** Your phone camera takes photos that are 3-8 MB (3,000-8,000 KB). The TNPSC portal requires photos between 20 KB and 50 KB.

**The fix:**
1. Go to SmartToolsWala.com in your browser
2. Open "Compress Image to 50KB"
3. Upload your original passport photo
4. Download the compressed result (35-48 KB)
5. Upload the compressed file to TNPSC portal

The whole process takes under 2 minutes.

## Mistake 2: Uploading a Signature That's Too Large

**The error:** Same "file size exceeds" message, but for the signature upload field.

**Why it happens:** People take a photo of their signature with their phone camera. The camera produces a large file (often 2-4 MB). TNPSC requires signatures between 10 KB and 20 KB.

**The fix:**
1. Crop your signature photo in Gallery to show just the signature
2. Go to SmartToolsWala.com
3. Open "Compress Image to 20KB"
4. Upload the cropped signature photo
5. Download (12-18 KB is typical for signatures)
6. Upload to TNPSC portal

## Mistake 3: Uploading a PNG File

**The error:** "Invalid file type. Only JPG/JPEG files are accepted."

**Why it happens:** Some phones (especially iPhones) save photos in HEIC format, not JPG. Screenshots are saved as PNG. Some candidates accidentally upload these instead of JPG.

**The fix:**
- When you use SmartToolsWala to compress your photo, the output is automatically converted to JPG format. So if your original is a PNG, HEIC, or any other format, just upload to our tool and the compressed download will be a proper JPG.
- Ensure the downloaded file ends in **.jpg** before uploading to TNPSC.

## Mistake 4: Uploading the Wrong Photo (Selfie Instead of Passport Photo)

**The error:** The portal may accept the file but your application could be rejected later during document verification.

**Why it happens:** Candidates use a casual selfie instead of a proper passport-style photo.

**What TNPSC requires:**
- Plain **white background** (not blue, not grey, not your bedroom wall)
- **Professional appearance** — no casual t-shirts, no sunglasses
- Face **directly facing camera** — not tilted, not side profile
- **Both ears visible** (no hair covering face)
- **Recent photo** (taken within last 6 months)

**The fix:** Take a proper passport-style photo before the application deadline. Go to a studio, or take one at home against a white wall in natural daylight with formal clothing.

## Mistake 5: Photo Is Below the Minimum Size

**The error:** "File size is too small. Minimum file size is 20 KB."

**Why it happens:** Over-compression. Using a too-small original. Starting with an already-compressed WhatsApp image.

**The fix:**
- Always start from your original camera photo, never from a WhatsApp-received image
- Take the original photo with your phone's HIGHEST quality camera setting
- If using our tool, your compressed result will automatically stay above 20 KB as long as you start with a good original

## Mistake 6: Uploading a Blurry Signature

**The error:** The portal accepts the file, but during physical verification, officers flag the signature as unclear.

**Why it happens:** Taking the signature photo at an angle, with flash, in poor lighting, or with a shaky hand.

**The fix:**
- Place the signature paper on a flat table
- Hold the phone directly above the paper (straight down, not at an angle)
- Use natural window light (NO flash)
- Tap the phone screen to focus on the signature
- Take 5 photos and pick the sharpest one
- Crop before compressing

## Mistake 7: Not Saving Your Compressed Files Before the Session Expires

**Why it happens:** TNPSC portals have session timeouts. Candidates sometimes compress photos in the middle of filling the form and lose the files when the session expires or the browser crashes.

**The fix:**
- **Compress all required photos BEFORE opening the TNPSC portal**
- Save both the compressed photo (35-48 KB) and signature (12-18 KB) to a clearly named folder on your phone
- Only then open the TNPSC portal and fill the form
- Have the files ready to upload when you reach the photo uploads section

## Pre-Submission Verification Checklist

Before clicking "Submit/Pay" on your TNPSC application, verify:

**Photo:**
- [ ] Format: .jpg
- [ ] Size: 20 KB to 50 KB
- [ ] Background: White
- [ ] Face: Clearly visible, front-facing
- [ ] Recent: Taken within last 6 months

**Signature:**
- [ ] Format: .jpg
- [ ] Size: 10 KB to 20 KB
- [ ] Background: White paper
- [ ] Signature lines: Dark and clearly visible

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The application shows my photo correctly but shows an error for the signature. Both were compressed the same way.
Double-check the signature file size. It must be between 10 KB and 20 KB — not 20-50 KB like the photo. Signature and photo have different size requirements. Make sure you used the 20KB compressor for the signature.

### I corrected my photo and re-uploaded, but the portal still shows the old photo in preview. What should I do?
Clear your browser cache (or press Ctrl+Shift+R to force refresh) after re-uploading. Some portals show a cached preview until the page is refreshed.

### My form is complete but I can't find the payment option. Is the photo upload related?
Usually not — the payment page comes after all required fields (including photos) are validated. If you're stuck and can't proceed, try clicking "Preview" to see which fields might still have errors.
