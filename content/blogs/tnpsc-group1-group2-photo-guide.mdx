TNPSC Group 1 and Group 2 examinations are among the most prestigious government job opportunities in Tamil Nadu. Group 1 leads to positions like District Collector, Deputy Superintendent of Police, and Tahsildar. Group 2 includes positions in civil service and cooperative service.

The competition is intense — tens of thousands of candidates apply for each notification. With such high stakes, a simple technical error like a wrong photo format can be devastating. This guide ensures your photo and signature uploads are perfect.





## TNPSC Group 1 and Group 2 Key Differences

Before jumping into photo requirements, understand the basic differences:

**TNPSC Group 1:**
- For candidates with degree or bachelor's qualification
- Includes interview (for most posts)
- Applications through one-time registration on TNPSC website

**TNPSC Group 2:**
- Divided into Group 2A (no interview) and Group 2 (with interview)
- For bachelor's degree holders
- Also through one-time registration

**Photo Requirements:** Both Group 1 and Group 2 use the TNPSC one-time registration system. Photo and signature are uploaded once and used for all future applications. This makes getting it right the first time extremely important.

## One-Time Registration: Why Photo Quality Matters More

TNPSC uses a one-time registration (OTR) system. When you register once, your photo and signature are stored and used for all subsequent exam applications.

This means:
- The same photo appears on every hall ticket you receive
- The same photo is used for verification at exam centers
- If the photo quality is poor, you're stuck with it for all future applications (unless you update it)

**This makes it worthwhile to spend extra time getting the best possible photo for TNPSC OTR.**

## Step-by-Step: TNPSC Photo Preparation

### Getting the Right Photo

**Studio option (recommended for TNPSC):**
Since this photo will be used for potentially years of applications, invest in a proper studio photo. Tell the studio photographer:
- "TNPSC registration photo chahiye" (or in Tamil: "TNPSC registration ku photo")
- White background
- Clear face
- Digital file in JPG format

A good studio photo compresses clean and clear to 50 KB. For an important exam like Group 1, this is worth ₹50-100.

**Home option:**
If photography at home:
1. White background — white wall, white bedsheet
2. Natural daylight from window — NO flash
3. Formal clothing (clean, collared shirt for men at minimum)
4. Both ears visible
5. Neutral expression
6. Rear camera, not selfie camera - use timer or friend to click

### Compression Process

1. Open SmartToolsWala in Chrome browser
2. Go to "Compress Image to 50KB"
3. Upload your high-quality original photo (2-8 MB is ideal)
4. Download the compressed result (35-48 KB)
5. Open the file and zoom to 150% — your face should be completely clear

### Signature Preparation

For TNPSC signature:
1. Sign your name boldly on white A4 paper
2. Use blue ballpoint pen
3. Sign slightly larger than usual
4. Photograph directly above in natural daylight
5. Crop to just the signature plus small border
6. Compress to 10-20 KB using SmartToolsWala's 20KB tool

## Interview Stage Photos (Group 1 and Group 2 with Interview)

If your TNPSC exam requires an interview stage, you will need:
- A fresh set of photographs for the interview call letter
- Physical passport photos for submission to the interview board
- Documents properly attested and arranged in the TNPSC-specified format

For the interview stage, invest in professional studio photos specifically for this purpose. The interview is the final stage — presentation matters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I update my photo after submitting TNPSC one-time registration?
TNPSC periodically opens editing windows for registered candidates to update details including photos. Watch for announcements on tnpsc.gov.in. These windows are usually brief and announced with short notice.

### My TNPSC profile photo looks different from what I uploaded. Why?
The portal might display a slightly different preview due to its internal cropping or resizing. The actual file stored is your original uploaded file. Don't judge quality from the portal preview thumbnail.

### Does TNPSC verify original photos against uploaded ones at exam centers?
Yes. Invigilators at exam centers check your hall ticket (which shows your uploaded photo) against your face. Make sure your current appearance is reasonably similar to your uploaded photo. Major changes (significant weight change, hair color change) could potentially cause confusion.

### I'm applying for TNPSC from abroad. Does everything still work the same way?
Yes — TNPSC accepts applications from candidates regardless of current location. Upload requirements are the same. Note that some examinations may have residency requirements for Tamil Nadu, so verify eligibility for your specific exam.
