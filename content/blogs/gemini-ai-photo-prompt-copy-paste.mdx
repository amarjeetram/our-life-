## Pro Tips for the Best Gemini AI Images

To get the absolute most out of Gemini AI, apply these professional optimization techniques:

1.  **Specify the Camera & Lens:** Adding details like *“shot on 85mm lens, f/1.8 aperture”* tells the AI to create a beautiful, professional background blur (bokeh) rather than a flat image.
2.  **Describe the Lighting:** Instead of just saying “good lighting,” use photographic terms like *“golden hour sun,” *“volumetric side-lighting,” *“soft diffuse light,”* or *“neon dual-tone contrast.”*
3.  **Command Face and Texture Integrity:** If you want a realistic look, always include terms like *“detailed skin pores,” *“realistic skin textures,”* and *“lifelike eye reflections.”*
4.  **Use Negative Prompts:** If you are using a tool that supports negative prompts, exclude terms like *“deformed hands, extra fingers, cartoonish, low resolution, blurry, text watermark”* to keep your renders clean.
