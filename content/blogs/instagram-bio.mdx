## Expert Formatting Tips

When optimizing your **instagram bio**, formatting is just as important as the text itself. Here are the top four design guidelines to format your profile:

1. **Use Line Breaks**: Instead of writing a long horizontal paragraph, split your credentials into neat vertical lines. This fits mobile screens perfectly.
2. **Leverage Unicode Symbols**: Symbols like stars (★), crowns (👑), arrows (→), and dividers (|) frame your text lines beautifully.
3. **Include a Clear Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Direct users to your link with down arrows (👇) or explicit pointers.
4. **Use Custom Fonts Sparingly**: Use styled scripts only for name tags or key headings to maintain readability.
