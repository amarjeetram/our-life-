## Why Does TNPSC Reject Large Photos?

When you take a photo with your mobile phone or a digital camera, the photo looks great. It has very high quality. But, a high-quality photo comes with a big "file size." It might be 5 MB (Megabytes) or even 10 MB.

The computers at the TNPSC office cannot store millions of 10 MB dark and huge files. It would fill up all their space. It would make their website very, very slow. Because of this, TNPSC makes a strong rule: **"All photos must be very small in size."**

Usually, they ask for a photo that is under 50 KB (Kilobytes). 50 KB is much, much smaller than 5 MB. If your photo is 5 MB, the uploading page will turn red. It will say an error message. It will tell you to upload a smaller file.

## 7 Big Mistakes Students Make with TNPSC Photos

Many students feel angry because TNPSC rejects their forms later. To stay safe, please do not make these silly mistakes! Read this list carefully.

1. **Using a Selfie:** Do not take a selfie. Selfies twist your face shape. Your arm might show. Always ask someone else to click the photo from a distance.
2. **Busy Backgrounds:** Do not stand in front of trees, cars, or colorful curtains. The background must be simple and white.
3. **Wearing Glasses with Glare:** If you wear seeing glasses, make sure the camera flash does not bounce off them. If TNPSC cannot see your eyes, you fail. Dark sunglasses are totally banned.
4. **Old Photos:** Do not use a photo from three years ago when you looked different. Use a new photo taken within the last 3 or 4 months.
5. **Too Blurry:** Do not compress it too much! If you compress a photo to 5 KB just for fun, it will look like a bunch of fuzzy dots. Keep it around 40 KB for faces.
6. **Blue Signatures on Dark Paper:** The signature paper must be pure white. If it looks gray or yellow, it is bad.
7. **Wrong Orientation:** Make sure the photo is standing up tall (Portrait). Make sure the signature is lying down wide (Landscape). Do not upload them upside down!
