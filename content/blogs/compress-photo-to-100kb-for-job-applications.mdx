## Why Job Portals Ask for 100KB Photos

Job portals like Naukri, Indeed, LinkedIn, and government recruitment websites receive millions of applications. Each application includes a photo. If every photo was 4 MB, the website would need enormous (and expensive) storage space and the pages would load slowly for recruiters.

At 100 KB, photos load instantly for HR managers, the portal runs smoothly, and storage costs are manageable. It's a smart technical limit that benefits everyone — including you, since your application loads faster on the recruiter's screen.

## What Makes a Good Professional Job Photo?

Before worrying about file size, make sure your photo itself is professional. A great job application photo should:

### Look Professional
- Wear formal or business-casual clothing (avoid casual t-shirts)
- Dark jacket, formal shirt, or saree for women
- Neat, clean appearance

### Have the Right Background
- **Plain white or light grey background** for corporate jobs
- Avoid outdoor backgrounds, busy rooms, or colorful patterns
- The background should make you stand out, not distract from you

### Show Clear Facial Features
- Face the camera directly
- Smile naturally (a genuine, relaxed smile makes a great impression)
- Eyes should be open and clearly visible
- No dark sunglasses
- Good lighting on your face — no half-shadow

### Be Recent
Use a photo taken within the last 6 months. Recruiters and HR managers need to be able to recognize you when you come for an interview.

## Taking a Professional Photo at Home

You don't need an expensive photo studio. A professional job photo can be taken at home with just your smartphone.

**What you need:**
- Your smartphone
- A plain white or light-colored wall
- Good natural light from a window
- Clean, formal clothes

**Steps:**
1. Stand 1.5 to 2 meters from the wall (enough distance so you don't cast a shadow on it)
2. Face a window — the natural light should illuminate your face from the front
3. Have a friend take the photo at eye level (not from below — that looks odd!)
4. Take 10 to 15 photos and choose the best one
5. The photo should show your face and the top of your shoulders

The original photo will be 2-5 MB. Now you need to compress it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I use a photo with or without spectacles?
If you wear spectacles in daily life and would wear them to an interview, include them in your photo. If you only wear them occasionally, either is fine. The most important thing is that the photo looks like you normally look.

### Can I use a photo taken at a party or event?
Generally, no. Crop the photo to show only your face and shoulders against a plain background only if the original photo has neutral lighting and a clean background. Otherwise, take a fresh photo specifically for the application.

### The portal is asking for "passport size photo" for a job. What does that mean?
This typically means a formal headshot (face and shoulders) taken against a white background — similar to a passport photo, but the file size requirement will be specified separately. Follow the file size limit listed on the portal.

### I need one photo for multiple different job portals with different size limits. What should I do?
Start with one high-quality original photo (4-5 MB). Use our tool to create multiple versions: one at 50 KB, one at 100 KB, one at 200 KB. Keep all versions saved on your phone so you can upload the right size to each portal.
