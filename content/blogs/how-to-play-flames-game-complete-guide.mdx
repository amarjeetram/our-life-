## What is the FLAMES Game?

FLAMES is a classic pencil-and-paper game played between two people (usually a boy and a girl) to predict the nature of their relationship. It has been a playground favourite across India, Southeast Asia, and beyond for decades.

The word **FLAMES** stands for:

| Letter | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **F** | Friends |
| **L** | Lovers |
| **A** | Affection |
| **M** | Marriage |
| **E** | Enemies |
| **S** | Siblings |

The game uses the names of two people and eliminates letters one by one using a counting algorithm. The last letter standing is your "destiny"!

## Step-by-Step: How to Play FLAMES Game

### Step 1: Write Both Names

Write your full name and the other person's full name, one below the other.

**Example:**
- Name 1: **SIDDHARTH**
- Name 2: **KIARA**

### Step 2: Cancel the Common Letters

This is the most important step. Cross out every letter that appears in **both** names. You cancel only the matching letters — not all occurrences.

**How to cancel:**
- Go through each letter in Name 1.
- If it also appears in Name 2, cross it out from both names.
- Cross out only one matching letter at a time (minimum of the two counts).

**Example:**
- SIDDHARTH has: S, I, D, D, H, A, R, T, H
- KIARA has: K, I, A, R, A

**Common letters:**
- **I** → cancel one I from each name
- **A** → cancel one A from each name
- **R** → cancel one R from each name

After cancelling:
- SIDDHARTH → S, D, D, H, **T**, H (remaining = 5 letters: S, D, D, H, T, H)
- KIARA → K, **A** (remaining = 2 letters: K, A)

> **Pro Tip:** Always cancel carefully, letter by letter. Many beginners make mistakes at this step!

### Step 3: Count the Remaining Letters

Add up all the remaining (uncrossed) letters from both names combined.

**Example:**
- Remaining from SIDDHARTH: S, D, D, H, T, H = **6 letters**
- Remaining from KIARA: K, A = **2 letters**
- **Total = 6 + 2 = 8**

### Step 4: Apply the FLAMES Counting Algorithm

Now write the word **FLAMES** on paper:

```
F – L – A – M – E – S
```

Starting from **F**, count up to your total number (in our example: **8**). The letter you land on gets eliminated. Then continue counting from the next letter (skip the eliminated one). Keep going until only **one letter remains**.

**Example with count = 8:**

Round 1: F(1), L(2), A(3), M(4), E(5), S(6), F(7), L(8) → **L** is eliminated
Remaining: F – A – M – E – S

Round 2: A(1), M(2), E(3), S(4), F(5), A(6), M(7), E(8) → **E** is eliminated
Remaining: F – A – M – S

Round 3: S(1), F(2), A(3), M(4), S(5), F(6), A(7), M(8) → **M** is eliminated
Remaining: F – A – S

Round 4: S(1), F(2), A(3), S(4), F(5), A(6), S(7), F(8) → **F** is eliminated
Remaining: A – S

Round 5: A(1), S(2), A(3), S(4), A(5), S(6), A(7), S(8) → **S** is eliminated
Remaining: **A** 🎉

### Step 5: Reveal Your Destiny!

The last remaining letter is your result!

In our example: **A = Affection** 💛

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is the FLAMES game accurate?

No — and yes! The FLAMES game is **purely for fun and entertainment**. It uses a deterministic mathematical algorithm, so the same two names always give the same result. However, it is NOT a real predictor of relationships. Think of it as a fun ice-breaker or a nostalgic game, not a scientific love test!

### 2. Can I play FLAMES with two boys' names or two girls' names?

Absolutely! The FLAMES game works for **any two names**, regardless of gender. The algorithm doesn't care — it just processes the letters and gives you a result.

### 3. What if both names are identical?

If both names are completely the same (e.g., "Rahul" and "Rahul"), all common letters cancel out and the remaining count would be 0. In most versions of the game (including our calculator), we default the count to **1** to avoid a zero error, giving you the letter F (Friends).

### 4. Does the order of names matter?

**Yes, it can!** Swapping names may give a different result because the cancellation process can work differently. Always try both orders if you're curious!

### 5. What does it mean if I get 'E' (Enemies)?

Getting "Enemies" doesn't mean you are doomed! It's a game result. Many real-life couples started as rivals before falling in love. Take it with a pinch of salt and have a laugh about it. ❤️

### 6. Can I play FLAMES with my celebrity crush?

Of course! That's part of the fun. Type in your name and your favourite celebrity's name and see what the universe has in store. We won't judge! 🌟

### 7. How is FLAMES played in school?

In school, FLAMES is traditionally played by writing names on paper, crossing out common letters, counting the remaining ones, and then using that count to eliminate letters from the word FLAMES one by one until only one letter is left. The surviving letter reveals your "destiny" with the other person.
