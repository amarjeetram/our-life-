India's competitive exam season brings millions of students to online registration portals. Whether it's UPSC, SSC, IBPS, railways, or state exams, the registration process always includes a strict photo upload step.

And for many exams, especially the toughest and most prestigious ones, the file size limit is set at the minimum: **20 KB**.

This guide will help you reduce any photo to 20 KB quickly and correctly, so your application goes through on the very first try.





## Which Exams Require 20KB Photos?

The 20 KB requirement is most common for:

- **SSC exams** (signature: 10-20 KB, photo: 20-50 KB)
- **IBPS PO and Clerk** (signature: 10-20 KB)
- **State exam portals** (Bihar, UP, Rajasthan boards)
- **RRB NTPC and Group D** (signature: 10-20 KB)
- **Defense and paramilitary** (signature: 10-20 KB)

While the passport photo is usually allowed up to 50 KB, the **signature** for almost all of these exams is capped at 20 KB.

## What Happens If Your File Is Over 20 KB?

The portal will show an error message like:
- "File size too large. Maximum allowed: 20 KB"
- "Upload failed. Please upload a smaller file"
- "Invalid file size"

Your application cannot proceed until you upload a correctly sized file. The portal will not accept 21 KB, 22 KB, or anything over the limit — even by 1 KB.

## Tips for Achieving Clean 20 KB Results

**Tip 1: Good originals compress better**
A sharp, well-lit original photo gives a cleaner result at 20 KB than a dark, blurry one. Always start with the best possible original.

**Tip 2: Crop before compress**
For signatures and isolated objects, crop the photo to remove unnecessary background BEFORE compressing. Less image area = easier to hit 20 KB with better quality.

**Tip 3: Use JPG format**
JPG compresses much smaller than PNG. If your photo is a PNG, convert it to JPG before or during compression. Our tool handles this automatically.

**Tip 4: Check in good lighting**
View the compressed photo on a bright screen in good light. What looks clear on a bright phone screen will be visible to the examiner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My exam allows 10-20 KB for signatures and my compressed file is 9 KB. Will it be rejected?
Yes — if the minimum is 10 KB, a 9 KB file will be rejected. Start with a higher-resolution original or crop slightly less tightly. The more pixels in the original, the easier it is to stay within a KB range.

### Can the same compressed photo be used for next year's exam?
Most portals require "recent" photographs (within 6 months). Check the current exam notification for exact requirements regarding photo recency.

### I compressed to 20 KB but the examiner at the center said it looked faded. What went wrong?
Likely the original was taken in poor light. Artificial light (especially yellow light from bulbs) makes skin tone and ink look pale. Always photograph in natural daylight near a window.
