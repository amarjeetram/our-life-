# The History of “Ship Names”

The idea of combining names became popular through **internet fandom culture and celebrity relationships**.

## The “Bennifer” Effect

In the early 2000s, the relationship between **Ben Affleck and Jennifer Lopez** created the famous nickname **Bennifer**. The media loved it, and the trend spread worldwide.

## The Rise of “Shipping”

The term **shipping** comes from the word **relationship**. Fans of movies and TV shows started creating names for characters they wanted to see together.

Examples include:

* Dramione (Draco + Hermione)
* Roschel (Ross + Rachel)

These fan-made names quickly became part of online culture.

## The Indian Trend

In India, the trend became extremely popular after celebrity weddings.

Examples include:

* **Virushka** (Virat Kohli + Anushka Sharma)
* **DeepVeer** (Deepika Padukone + Ranveer Singh)

These names became trending hashtags and social media brands.
